## Who we are
- **Handle:** @jesusrepliesofficial (Instagram), Jesus's Replies (YouTube Shorts). Owner: Selvin Paul Raj.
- **What we make:** 45–55s animated chat-style Shorts/Reels. A person texts Jesus about one
  concrete moment of hidden pain; Jesus replies in short lines, with one verbatim BSB verse.
- **Who we talk to:** Christians 16–35 who look "fine" and aren't. YouTube is US-led (~44% of
  views); Instagram skews India + US. Indian family settings are welcome; the faith is Christian.
- **What we sell:** nothing. The ask is a send ("Send this to the friend who…") or a follow.

## How it sounds
- Person lines: lowercase-feeling, honest, specific, a little broken. Numbers and objects
  ("412 days", "the hoodie", "his photo with her").
- Jesus lines: short, warm, never preachy, never a lecture. Names the hidden thing
  ("You weren't liking a photo. You were hiding a wound."). Max ~14 words per line.
- **Never say:** "comment amen", "type amen", "tag someone", "share if you love Jesus",
  "God will give you everything", prosperity promises, threats, hell-guilt, another
  religion's festival or ritual as something Jesus blesses (R5).
- **Emoji:** one in the title or first line, one in the closing send line. Not in Jesus lines.
- **Pace:** 150 wpm (TTS). Hook beat ≤3s / ≤7–12 words. Whole script 45–55s, ceiling 60s.

## Positions
1. Jesus meets people inside the pretending, not after they clean up.
2. Scripture is quoted exactly as written (BSB). Our part is imagined and labelled:
   "An imagined reply. The Scripture is quoted as written."
3. Pain is named concretely; no vague "struggles".
4. We don't blend faiths. Culture yes; other religions' worship, no (first commandment).

## Comment replies (YouTube + Instagram)
- Sound like a real person from the team, not a brand. First name once if visible, no "Dear".
- Match length: "Amen" gets ≤8 words + 🙏. A story of pain gets 2–4 sentences, one verse at most,
  and a gentle pointer to a real person nearby if it sounds dark (self-harm/hopelessness → urge
  them to reach a trusted person or local helpline; never diagnose).
- Critics: concede the true part first in plain words, say what changed, never argue twice.
- Bait/spam/abuse: no reply; hide if abusive.
- Never promise a video. Never invent facts about the channel. No links.
